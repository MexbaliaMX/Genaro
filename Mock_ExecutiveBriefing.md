# Mock Executive Briefing Blueprint

Use this blueprint to storyboard and deliver executive-facing briefings within Genaro DFT 2.0. Design each section as a reusable module so it can render inside the Unified Command Dashboard, export to PDF, or power a live briefing deck.

## 1. Cover & Context
- Title block with crisis/topic name, time horizon, confidence index badge, and data freshness timestamp.  
- Snapshot metrics: sentiment delta, projected impact window, exposure level, risk tier.  
- Stakeholder routing tags (CEO, CMO, CFO) with one-click acknowledgment log.

## 2. Scenario Synopsis
- Narrative origin summary (source, trigger, amplification vectors) backed by citation links.  
- Timeline strip for the past 72 hours highlighting inflection points and agent interventions.  
- Competitive posture callout showing ally/competitor responses and signal strength.

## 3. Predictive Outlook
- Probability bands for escalation vs. containment visualized via gauges or fan charts.  
- Financial projection tiles tied to market volatility, revenue at risk, or funnel KPIs.  
- Audience sentiment matrix segmented by investors, customers, regulators, employees.

## 4. Prescriptive Actions
- Ranked action cards (A/B/C) with expected effectiveness, effort score, and lift percentage.  
- Resource checklist (teams, budget, channels) plus dependency warnings.  
- HITL approval status with approver name, timestamp, and pending items.

## 5. Execution Tracker
- Gantt-style timeline with status (planned, in-flight, completed) and owner assignments.  
- Channel performance indicators: CTR, engagement shift, response rate, cost burn.  
- Alert panel for deviations beyond tolerance thresholds, each linked to remediation playbooks.

## 6. Governance & Notes
- Compliance checklist aligned with policy/legal/data requirements and completion state.  
- Open risks, mitigation owners, and due dates with risk heat scoring.  
- Quick links to detailed agent reports, sandbox simulations, and archived briefings.

## 7. Next Check-In
- Scheduled review date, time, and responsible executive; include calendar deep link.  
- Required decisions summary with countdown timers for expirations.  
- Post-mortem prompt and storage location once scenario closes.

## 8. Delivery & Recordkeeping
- Export options: dashboard slide, PDF packet, secured email digest.  
- Distribution log documenting who viewed the briefing and when.  
- Audit reference pointing to preserved datasets and evidence chains used in the briefing.
