# 📑 Conversion Strategy Framework – Genaro DFT 2.0

---

## 1. Executive Rationale

En el mundo digital actual, medir solo clics o ventas es insuficiente. La verdadera conversión combina:

* **Resultados de marketing** (CTR, CPA, leads, ventas).
* **Impacto reputacional** (confianza, percepción, share of voice).
* **Eficiencia financiera** (ROI de cada dólar invertido en Ads).

**Genaro – DFT 2.0** conecta estas tres dimensiones en un único marco de conversión:
👉 **Del awareness → a la lealtad**, pasando por reputación y ROI financiero.

---

## 2. Personas & Roles

This framework is designed primarily for:

*   **Marcus (Performance Marketing Lead):** Who will use it to track and optimize ad spend against narrative and financial goals.
*   **Camila (Campaign/Comms Lead):** Who will use it to understand the reputational impact of their campaigns and make data-driven decisions about messaging.
*   **David (Executive/Stakeholder):** Who will use the high-level metrics (like Narrative ROI) to understand the overall performance of the company's marketing and communications efforts.

---

## 3. Conversion Funnel (Embudo Narrativo + Financiero)

### Nivel 1 – Awareness

* **Qué medimos**: alcance, impresiones, share of voice.
* **Narrative KPIs**: número de narrativas en las que participamos, sentimiento inicial.
* **Ads KPIs**: CPM, impresiones, CTR base.

### Nivel 2 – Engagement

* **Qué medimos**: interacción con contenidos.
* **Narrative KPIs**: emociones dominantes, resonancia de mensajes.
* **Ads KPIs**: CTR, CPC, tasa de visualización de video.

### Nivel 3 – Conversion

* **Qué medimos**: acciones de negocio.
* **Narrative KPIs**: Δ Sentimiento entre audiencias que convirtieron.
* **Ads KPIs**: CPA, conversion rate, leads generados.

### Nivel 4 – Loyalty & Advocacy

* **Qué medimos**: retención y recomendación.
* **Narrative KPIs**: confianza en marca, NPS, menciones positivas.
* **Ads KPIs**: Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), tasa de recompra.

---

## 4. Key Metrics for CMOs & CFOs

* **Cost per Conversion (CPCv):** gasto total / conversiones logradas.
* **Cost per Sentiment Shift (CPS):** gasto / puntos porcentuales ganados en sentimiento positivo.
* **Narrative ROI:** Δ reputación ponderada por share of voice / inversión en Ads.
* **Conversion Sentiment Lift (CSL):** diferencia de sentimiento entre convertidos y no convertidos.
* **Return on Narrative Investment (RONI):** cuánto valor reputacional se obtiene por cada dólar invertido.

---

## 5. Playbook de Tácticas de Conversión

### Táctica 1 – Awareness → Conversion

* Aprovechar narrativas trending con Ads dirigidos.
* Mensajes alineados con emociones dominantes detectadas por Genaro.

### Táctica 2 – Negative → Neutral

* Campañas de transparencia y educación.
* Mitigación de crisis: mensajes que desactivan percepciones negativas.

### Táctica 3 – Neutral → Positive

* Testimoniales y casos de éxito amplificados en Ads.
* Incentivos emocionales: campañas aspiracionales y de confianza.

### Táctica 4 – Positive → Action

* Call-to-actions fuertes (compra, registro, inversión).
* Ofertas exclusivas para segmentos ya favorables.

### Táctica 5 – Conversion → Loyalty

* Programas de fidelización y recompra.
* Medición de NPS + sentimiento post-compra.
* Reinyección de insights al modelo de Genaro para optimizar próximos ciclos.

---

## 6. CFO & CMO Alignment

* **CMO ve:** engagement, percepción, narrativa.
* **CFO ve:** inversión, ROI financiero, eficiencia en Ads.
* **Genaro unifica:** ambos hablan el mismo lenguaje → **dinero gastado → impacto narrativo → resultados financieros**.

## 7. Genaro's Role in the Conversion Framework

Genaro is the intelligent agent that operationalizes this framework by:

*   **Automating Metric Calculation:** Genaro automatically calculates the key metrics, such as "Cost per Sentiment Shift (CPS)" and "Narrative ROI," by correlating ad spend data with narrative analysis.
*   **Recommending Conversion Tactics:** Based on the current state of a narrative and the desired outcome, Genaro will recommend the most appropriate "Playbook Tactic" to use. For example, if a narrative has high awareness but low sentiment, Genaro might recommend the "Negative → Neutral" tactic.
*   **Generating Insights for Optimization:** Genaro will proactively provide insights and recommendations for optimizing ad spend. For example, it might suggest reallocating budget from a low-performing campaign to a high-performing one, based on their respective impacts on the narrative.
*   **Simulating Tactic Impact:** Before a tactic is implemented, Genaro can simulate its potential impact in the "Sandbox Studio," providing a forecast of the expected change in sentiment, share of voice, and other key metrics.

---

## 8. Dashboard & Visualization

The Conversion Strategy Framework will be visualized in the **Advertising Dashboard** through the following components:

*   **Conversion Funnel Visualization:** A visual representation of the four-level conversion funnel, showing the number of users at each stage and the conversion rates between stages.
*   **Narrative ROI Dashboard:** A dedicated section of the dashboard that displays the key metrics for CMOs & CFOs, such as "Cost per Sentiment Shift (CPS)" and "Narrative ROI."
*   **Tactic Performance View:** A view that shows the performance of the different "Playbook Tactics" that have been implemented, allowing users to compare their effectiveness.
*   **Genaro's Recommendations:** A dedicated area where Genaro's recommendations for conversion optimization are displayed.
